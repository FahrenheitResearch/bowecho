# BowEcho v0.29.2 — Faster Loops, Deeper Storms

v0.29.2 is a big one. Long velocity loops now stay fast on replay instead of
re-doing all their work; satellite goes full true-color; WRF becomes a
first-class model you can browse, sound, and cross-section like any other; the
hurricane layer grows real forecast dots with official per-point intensity;
and a wave of quality-of-life features lands — background alerts so you never
miss a warning, storm-track swaths, obs history, radar outages, and more. Most
of it was built and proven live against Super Typhoon BAVI-26 on the Guam
radar.

## Loops that stay fast

The heavy work of a velocity loop — decoding volumes and dealiasing each tilt —
now happens **once** and is reused on every replay and product toggle.

- **Dealias once, reuse forever.** Each velocity tilt is dealiased a single
  time; replaying the loop, toggling products, or opening a second pane reuse
  the cached result instead of re-running the ~200 ms dealiaser. The memo now
  **scales with your RAM budget and pane count**, so a full long loop (256+
  frames) never evicts and re-dealiases partway through — the old "fast for a
  few loops, then slow again" is gone.
- **Re-load without re-decode.** Decoded volumes are cached (LRU), so
  re-loading a loop you already have is near-instant.
- **Faster archive loads.** Archive-loop decode is parallelized and streams
  frames as they arrive.
- **Pick your RAM.** A new history-budget control (4–32 GiB) plus a "⚠ RAM
  cap" readout in the loop bar — no more silently trimming a 200-frame load
  down to what fit in a fixed 8 GiB.

## Velocity loops behave

- **No more mid-loop flip to reflectivity.** A velocity loop that hit a frame
  without velocity used to silently revert to reflectivity for the rest of the
  loop; it now holds velocity and restores it on the next capable frame.
- **Dual-pane stays put.** A following velocity pane no longer shows the last
  stretch of the loop as reflectivity, and switching to dual-pane no longer
  re-does the dealiasing.
- **No 1-frame flash.** The brief reflectivity flash when a loop stepped onto a
  velocity-less partial is gone.

## Hurricane forecast track — dots + official intensity

The tropical layer's cone now carries **forecast dots** stepping out along the
track, each colored by its Saffir–Simpson **category at that forecast time**.

- **Real per-point intensity.** For Atlantic / East-Pacific storms the dots use
  NHC's Forecast/Advisory (TCM); for the West Pacific, Indian Ocean, and
  Southern Hemisphere they use the **JTWC** warning — so the dots fade from,
  say, Category 5 down to a tropical storm across the forecast, exactly as the
  official center predicts.
- **Hover for the numbers.** Each dot shows its valid time (UTC + your local
  time) and max sustained wind.
- The panel also surfaces the storm-motion note and the GBVTD wavenumber-1
  eyewall asymmetry where available.

All forecast feeds here are free and keyless.

## Satellite in true color

Beyond colorized infrared, BowEcho now builds **true / natural-color
composites** from multi-band satellite:

- **GOES ABI** natural-color and GeoColor-style composites (and more) — deep
  blue ocean, tan land, white cloud, in real geography.
- **Himawari AHI** true color for the West Pacific — you can see the eye of a
  typhoon in natural color, not grayscale.

## WRF is now a first-class model

BowEcho ingests WRF/NetCDF directly into the model store, so a WRF run browses,
sounds, cross-sections, and plots like HRRR or any other model.

- Import a **whole folder** or a **single file** (no forced batches).
- Two paths: a light NetCDF/wrfout import (2D surface + sounding volumes) and a
  heavier full-diagnostics path (CAPE / severe set via wrf-core).

## Model plots (rusty-weather v0.2.3)

The upstream v0.2.3 features are now wired into the UI:

- **Custom plot domains** — draw a box (or rotate a corner) to render a model
  field through rusty-weather's native plot pipeline over your own area.
- **Editable color tables** — edit a field's palette and it persists across
  restarts.

## Quality of life

A batch of community-requested features:

- **Never miss a warning.** Warning polling and the alert sound now fire even
  when BowEcho is **minimized / in the background**, on a schedule independent
  of the window — so a new tornado warning still sounds when you're not looking
  at the app. Plus an optional "radar updated" chime.
- **Custom warning feed + faster refresh.** Point BowEcho at your own warning
  URL (NWS CAP/GeoJSON or VTEC text), and set the refresh interval yourself
  (down to 5 s, was a fixed 30 s).
- **Storm-track swaths.** "Max REF" and "Max VEL" overlays accumulate the
  per-gate maximum across the loop, so you can see where a storm has *been*
  without scrubbing back.
- **Surface-obs history.** Right-click a surface station → pin a timeline
  scrubber of its temperature/dew point/wind/pressure; archive-aware.
- **Radar status & outages.** See which radars are down and any operator
  message, on a map badge and in a status panel.

## GBVTD (TC winds)

- **Better intensity.** The retrieval now removes the storm-motion wind from
  the fit, raising a Cat-5's estimate closer to reality, and adds a
  **wavenumber-1 eyewall asymmetry** term (with a panel readout).
- **No more garbage numbers.** Physical and geometry gates kill the old failure
  modes (thousands of knots off-eye, wildly high at the eye) — an unphysical
  click now reports "no coherent circulation" instead of a nonsense number.
- Click-to-place the eye, a **Clear** button, and a panel close control.

## Fixes

- **Tropical layer no longer flashes "no active."** A slow or unavailable
  source (GDACS carries the West Pacific) while the other basin was quiet used
  to show a false all-clear for up to 10 minutes; it now shows a spinner /
  "retrying," keeps any storms already on screen, and re-checks every 20 s
  until it succeeds.
- **Cone of uncertainty draws again** — and stays drawn on wide West-Pacific
  storms even when you zoom in (it was being culled as if it crossed the
  dateline).

## Under the hood

- Dealias-grid and decode caches; WRF ingest via wrf-core + a vendored NetCDF
  reader; rusty-weather v0.2.3.
