# BowEcho v0.30.0 — "New Countries, Clean Foundations"

Two brand-new radar countries, one-press storm satellite views, smarter WRF
imports, and the largest internal cleanup in BowEcho's history — with zero
intended behavior change from the refactor.

## 🇪🇸 Spain joins the map (AEMET)

AEMET opened Spain's volumetric radar network in June 2026, and BowEcho
supports it day-one: all **11 operational sites**, including **Artenara and
Buenavista Norte in the Canary Islands** — reflectivity and velocity at
10-minute cadence. Under the hood this took teaching our HDF5 reader AEMET's
newer file dialect (version-2 object headers, now checksum-verified) and
correcting their nonstandard range encoding so data draws at true distance.
Data: AEMET, via EUMETNET ORD (CC BY 4.0).

## 🇷🇴 Romania goes native dual-pol (ANM)

Romania upgrades from the shared European feed to ANM's own open data:
**ZDR, KDP, and RhoHV** join reflectivity and velocity across all 7 Romanian
radars at 5-minute cadence — differential moments the shared feed doesn't
carry. Data: Administrația Națională de Meteorologie (ANM) România.

## 🛰 One-press storm satellite

Every tropical cyclone card now has **🛰 Vis** and **🛰 IR** buttons next to
Focus: one press picks the covering satellite for that storm's basin
(Himawari-9 / GOES-East / GOES-West), pulls a native-resolution window
centered on the storm, and opens it — true color for Vis, Band-13
brightness temperature with your chosen enhancement for IR. Himawari IR
re-colors live when you switch enhancement palettes.

## 🌡 Solarpower07's complete temperature set

The v0.29.3 temperature palette gets its full upstream update: dedicated
tables for **250 / 500 / 700 / 850 mb** (each tuned to its level's range)
and a **°C-native surface table** with hard cuts at 0/30/40 °C. Fields pick
their level table automatically. Credit: Solarpower07 (WRF-Runner).

## 🧪 WRF imports you can actually read

Imported WRF runs used to list raw registry names (`wrf_swupt`…). Now **174
diagnostic variables** carry readable names, hover descriptions, and
sensible palettes (135 resolve real color ramps) — radiation budget, surface
energy, precip, snow, soil, PBL, convective maxima, and more. Applies to
already-imported runs — no re-import needed.

## 🎈 Upper air, unlocked

Imported WRF runs and downloaded models have always carried isobaric
volumes for soundings — now they're plottable maps: **Temperature,
Dewpoint, RH, Wind speed, and Height at 925 / 850 / 700 / 500 / 300 /
250 mb** appear right in the model field picker, wearing their
level-specific palettes. Works on already-imported runs — no re-import.

## 🧹 Under the hood: the great decomposition

`main.rs` shrank **14.5%** (79,177 → 67,694 lines) across seven byte-identical
module extractions (self-update, hazard geometry, hazard UI, product
selection, settings, satellite painting, geo helpers), each verified with
move-only diff review and full test parity — plus a ratchet test that fails
CI if the monolith ever grows again. Test suite grew from 1,790 to 1,833+
along the way with zero failures at any step.
