# BowEcho v0.30.4 — "Sharper Look"

Better-looking radar top to bottom: a rewritten 3D velocity view, a real
native-resolution cursor loupe, selectable radar sharpness, cleaner zoomed-out
radar, and an in-app guide caught up to everything the v0.30 series added.

## Cursor loupe now shows true native gates

The Field Loupe (the cursor magnifier) previously magnified the on-screen radar
image, so at high zoom it showed chunky blocks. It now samples the **actual
radar gates** — projecting each loupe pixel back to the radar's range/azimuth
and reading the real gate value, colored exactly like the map. You get crisp,
true-resolution detail at any magnification, matching the cursor readout
gate-for-gate. Works for reflectivity, velocity, and the dual-pol moments.

Alongside it: the loupe has a clean opaque backdrop (no more low-resolution map
ghosting through no-echo areas), the readout card no longer overlaps the loupe
when it flips to stay on screen, and you can **scroll to change the loupe's
magnification** (2x–20x) while it's open.

## 3D velocity, rewritten

Velocity in the 3D Volume Explorer used to render through a reflectivity-tuned
transfer function and looked like a structureless cloud. It now takes its 3D
**structure from reflectivity** and its **color from velocity**, so the storm's
real shape is painted with its motion — couplets and rotation read clearly. New
velocity controls (a reflectivity gate and a couplet-emphasis slider) and
presets (Broad flow / Couplet emphasis / Strong shear) replace the reflectivity
ones when a velocity product is loaded.

## Selectable radar sharpness

A new **Raster quality** setting (Settings, Display) — Standard / High / Ultra —
renders radar at higher internal resolution so gates are genuinely sharper when
you zoom in and in native screenshots. By default only the frame you are viewing
gets the treatment (loops stay light on memory); a power-user "apply to the
whole loop" option renders every loop frame at full quality, with a memory
estimate shown. Off by default.

## Cleaner zoomed-out radar

Radar textures now anti-alias correctly when zoomed out (crisp gates zoomed in
are unchanged), removing the moire pattern that showed at low zoom.

## In-app guide refreshed

The built-in Guide now documents the v0.30 series: WRF import and friendly
variable names, upper-air isobaric fields, the simulated-radar-from-WRF controls,
the Spain and Romania radar coverage, and the satellite enhancements.
