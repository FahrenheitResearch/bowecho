# BowEcho v0.30.5 — "Decades of Weather"

The model side of BowEcho grows up: browse and download NCAR climate datasets
from inside the app (including the ERA-20C reanalysis back to 1900), get the
full severe-weather suite on every kind of WRF file, process it several times
faster, auto-plot everything you import, and share your simulated radar as
real radar files.

## GDEX climate data browser

A new dataset browser (Model data window, "GDEX (NSF NCAR)") crawls the NSF
NCAR GDEX THREDDS catalogs in-app. Two datasets ship wired:

- **CONUS II** — the 4 km regional climate WRF runs (present-era and
  future-era), as post-processed NetCDF.
- **ERA-20C** — ECMWF's reanalysis of the entire 20th century (1900-2010),
  as GRIB-1 files, decoded natively in Rust. No external tools.

Browse the catalog tree, download whole files or server-side NCSS subsets
(pick variables, a bounding box, a single time), and completed downloads
import automatically. Downloads are **cancellable mid-stream** and resumable:
cancel keeps the partial file, and re-downloading picks up where it stopped
instead of restarting a multi-gigabyte pull.

## Severe weather everywhere WRF goes

- Raw `wrfout` files: the wrench import computes the full severe/thermo suite
  (CAPE flavors, CIN, LCL/LFC/EL, SRH, bulk shear, STP/SCP/EHI and more) for
  one file or hundreds.
- Post-processed climate WRF (CONUS II `wrf3d`): the same 16-field severe
  suite now computes from the derived TK/Z/P state — these files previously
  imported with no severe fields at all. Both eras work; the history-era
  files store height on a staggered grid and are handled transparently.
- `wrf2d` surface archives: the full catalog (186 fields on a real CONUS II
  file — accumulation, land, and flux planes) imports as labeled 2-D fields.
  Known limit: a handful of datasets in these files (10 m U wind, MUCAPE,
  SRH03 among them) are invisible to the current NetCDF reader and are
  skipped until an upstream reader fix lands.

## WRF processing is several times faster

The wrf-rust diagnostics engine was profiled on a real 800x800x79 inner
domain and rebuilt where it hurt: the effective-inflow-layer scan that ran
ten times per file now runs once, the depth-limited CAPE products and
equilibrium-level grids are parallelized, composites reuse cached CAPE and
DCAPE instead of recomputing them, and HDF5 chunks decompress in parallel.
Verified numerically against wrf-python with zero parity failures - and the
diagnostic workload runs about 3.3x faster. The app also stops paying a
half-minute NetCDF probe per raw wrfout before processing starts.

Accuracy went up alongside speed: parcel lifts above 1000 hPa, CIN layer
selection, vorticity map metrics, and several composite formulas were
corrected against wrf-python and the SPC definitions during a full audit of
the diagnostics engine.

## Auto-plot every field

After any model import (or on demand with "Plot all fields..."), BowEcho
batch-renders production-quality PNG plots — titles, colorbars, state and
country lines — of **every field at every forecast hour**, including the
925-250 mb upper-air levels, into `Pictures/BowEcho/plots/<model>/<run>/`,
one folder per field, one frame per hour, with an `index.json` manifest.
Point the importer at a folder of wrfouts before bed; wake up to plots.

## Share your simulated radar

The WRF simulated radar can now be exported ("Export CfRadial...") as
CfRadial-1 NetCDF volumes — the research-standard radar format. The files
re-open in BowEcho losslessly and load in standard radar tools. One file per
frame for loops.

## Quality of life

- The app opens at a sensible size for YOUR monitor: near-filling on a
  16:9 display, a large-but-sane window on ultrawides, instead of one fixed
  size for everyone.
- The "update available" chip now opens the in-app updater in Settings
  instead of sending you to a web page.
- The processed-datasets list in the Model window scrolls properly.
- The model sounding opens as a single docked panel beside the plot — no
  more duplicate windows or the plot collapsing when a sounding closes.
- Settings and styles persistence hardened: debounced writes, corrupt
  imports rejected, only valid backups kept.
- Radar ingest hardening across the board: decoder resource limits, bounded
  HTTP buffering, band-sensitive products fail closed without band evidence,
  and distinct local scan times are kept as separate loop frames.
