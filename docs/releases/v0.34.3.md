# BowEcho v0.34.3

This update refines archive workflows, sounding analysis, radar-product
controls, and WoFS visibility.

## Highlights

- Soundings can now float in a separate, fully resizable window and dock back
  into the workspace.
- Fit and Stretch sizing now work in both docked and floating soundings.
- Added Maximum Parcel Level (MPL) for SFC, ML, forecast-surface, and MU
  parcels.
- Corrected streamwiseness to use the squared horizontal-vorticity fraction.
- Ordinary US radar archives now automatically show NWS warnings valid at the
  displayed scan time, including single-scan archives. Returning live restores
  current-warning updates.
- Added persistent radar-product settings for Modern categorized or Classic
  compact layouts, extended products, and per-product tilt memory.
- Strengthened WoFS High visibility while preserving the published colors,
  transparency, placement, and georeference.
- Renamed warning-card headings to **Current warnings**.
- Prevented the sidebar edge control from covering sounding content.

Archive warning availability depends on the public NWS archive and remains
limited to supported US warning products.
